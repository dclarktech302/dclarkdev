## The "Neon Nights" Philosophy

### Core Principle
> **"Premium tech aesthetic with strategic warmth"**

Like walking through a cyberpunk city:
- Most signs are **cold neon blue/purple** (tech, professional)
- Occasional **warm orange lights** mark human spaces (bars, homes, personal)
- Creates **contrast and hierarchy**

### Tech Elements = Cold Neon
- Hero gradient
- Primary CTAs
- Portfolio cards
- Tech stack
- Experience cards
- Client highlights

**Why:** Positions you as cutting-edge, premium, enterprise-caliber

### Human Elements = Warm Orange
- About section (your story)
- Local services (community)
- Contact invitations
- "Let's Talk" buttons
- Timeline milestones

**Why:** Shows warmth, approachability, human connection
